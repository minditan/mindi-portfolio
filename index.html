AI Smart Glasses

Real-Time Speech-To-Text Smart Glasses for Accessibility

Project Overview

The AI Smart Glasses project is a wearable embedded system designed to provide real-time speech transcription directly within a user’s field of view. The device continuously captures spoken audio through an INMP441 MEMS digital microphone, where speech is sampled and transmitted to an ESP32 microcontroller for preprocessing before being sent to a speech recognition model. The recognized text is then rendered onto a compact waveguide/AR display module, projecting live captions onto the lens without obstructing the user’s natural vision.

By integrating embedded systems, wireless communication, artificial intelligence, and real-time software, the project creates an intuitive hands-free interface that allows users to read conversations as they occur. The goal is to achieve low-latency transcription while maintaining a lightweight, portable, and energy-efficient wearable platform suitable for everyday use.

Motivation

As someone who is hard of hearing, I have experienced firsthand the challenges of following conversations in noisy environments or situations where speech clarity is limited. This project was inspired by a desire to create technology that removes communication barriers rather than adapting to them.

Beyond accessibility, I wanted to pursue a project that combines my passion for both software engineering and embedded hardware design. Developing these smart glasses allows me to work across the full engineering stack—from circuit integration and embedded programming to AI inference and user interface design—while creating a product that has the potential to make a meaningful impact on everyday life.

My goal is to build technology that is not only technically innovative but also genuinely improves the way people communicate.

Impact & Applications

The AI Smart Glasses are designed to improve communication accessibility by providing instant speech captions directly within the user’s line of sight. Unlike traditional mobile captioning applications that require users to repeatedly look away from conversations, this wearable solution enables continuous eye contact and more natural interactions.

Potential applications include:

  • Accessibility for individuals who are hard of hearing or deaf
  • Real-time communication assistance in noisy environments
  • Language translation for multilingual conversations
  • Educational settings and classroom accessibility
  • Professional meetings and presentations
  • Everyday hands-free communication

By combining embedded hardware, artificial intelligence, and human-centered design, this project demonstrates how engineering can be leveraged to create technology that is both innovative and socially impactful.
